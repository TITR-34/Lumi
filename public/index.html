Add your restaurant

Step 1 — chat with Lumi. Describe your menu in your own words (Azerbaijani, English or Russian): dishes, prices, ingredients. You can keep chatting to add, change or remove dishes.

Step 2 — review & edit

Edit anything. Click the photo box to upload your own dish photo (stored in Supabase). Allergens: comma-separated from gluten, dairy, nuts, egg, fish, shellfish, soy, sesame · none = no allergens · empty = unknown (treated as unsafe for allergy requests).
